Biscuits

Oven: 450f

Bake: 15 min

Steps

  1. Slightly melt butter in frying pan
  2. Move pan off heat
  3. Mix in the rest of ingredients
  4. Form into balls and put on baking sheet with a bit of space between
  5. Bake and enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 3/4 teaspoon cream of tartar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup 5% fat Greek yogurt

Notes for next time: this recipe makes mini bread. Try chilling before baking, and adding 5 minutes to time.
