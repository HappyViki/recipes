Cookie Base

Oven: 350f

Bake: 20 min

Steps

  1. Chop 1 small gala apple
  2. Pan fry in butter, honey, and cinnamon until golden brown
  3. Melt butter for cookie base in frying pan
  4. Move pan off heat, make sure it's cool enough to touch
  5. Add the rest of cookie base ingredients, in that order
  6. Mix with spoon, then finish mixing with hands
  7. Form into balls and put on baking sheet with a bit of space between
  8. Press down on each ball and form into cups
  9. Put chopped apples in center
  10. Bake and enjoy!

Ingredients For Cookie Filling

  • 1 small gala apple
  • Dash cinnamon
  • Spoonful of honey

Ingredients For Cookie Base

  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
