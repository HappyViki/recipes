Banana Nut Cookies

Oven: 325f

Bake: 30 min

Makes: 9 cookies

Steps

  1. Combine ingredients
  2. May need to add more flour, depends on banana size
  3. Bake and enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 bananas, regular size
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
